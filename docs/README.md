# OctoAcme Project Management Playbook

Welcome to the OctoAcme process docs. This guide gives new team members a quick, practical view of how projects move from idea to learning.

## How OctoAcme runs projects

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative project lifecycle:

1. **Initiation** – Define the problem, goals, stakeholders, success metrics, and initial risks.
2. **Planning** – Break work into prioritized backlog items, align timelines, define quality expectations, and map dependencies.
3. **Execution & Tracking** – Deliver in small increments, use regular team rituals, track progress and risks, and keep quality checks in CI.
4. **Release** – Verify readiness, deploy safely, communicate outcomes, and prepare rollback plans.
5. **Retrospectives** – Review what worked, what did not, and convert lessons into owned action items.

## Core principles

- **Customer-first outcomes** over output
- **Iterative delivery** with small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership** across product, project, and engineering
- **Data-informed decisions** using metrics and feedback
- **Continuous improvement** through retrospectives and follow-through

## Core roles

- **Project Manager (PM):** coordinates plans, delivery rhythm, risks, and stakeholder communication
- **Product Manager (PdM):** defines outcomes, priorities, and success measures
- **Developers and QA:** build, validate, and ship reliable increments
- **UX/UI Designer:** designs user experiences, wireframes, and prototypes; ensures usability and accessibility
- **Business Analyst (BA):** elicits and documents requirements; bridges business and technical teams
- **DevOps Engineer:** owns CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, and deployment automation
- **QA Automation Engineer:** builds and maintains automated test suites; enforces quality gates in CI
- **Support/Operations (SRE):** monitors production health, coordinates incident response, and feeds learnings back into planning
- **Stakeholders/Sponsors:** align on priorities, decisions, and approvals
